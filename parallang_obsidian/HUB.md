# INTRO
- The players should be greeted by the game's philosophy. This means: "We believe that language learning shouldn't be too rigid or even necessarily structured. Learning should happen organically, with the learner picking up knowledge almost passively and accumulating it over time. Immersion is important for engagement and retention. This is how children learn their mother tongue, by being thrown into scenarios and exposed to the language passively."
	- Note: Repetition should still be a structured element
