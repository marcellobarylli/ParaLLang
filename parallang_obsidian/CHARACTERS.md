### The Lethargers

- I'd like to personify the way that in modern society, distraction and attention fragmentation is around every corner. Rather than simply doing something, we are always doing that thing _while also resisting the urge to follow another thing_. The Lethargers could be little gnome-like creatures that dance in the corner of your vision, throwing firecrackers and moving in a way that hijacks our reward centers. [#halfbaked](app://obsidian.md/index.html#halfbaked)
- Could be a gameplay feature somehow
