### Amsterdam Grant Pitch
- Netherlands, Amsterdam in particular, is a hub for internationals. A lot of expats I meet are actively learning Dutch, which I believe is essential for their integration. Current language learning apps seem to be limited in how well people learn to have actual conversations. LLMs are able to provide conversational practice, but with the added bonus of an immersive world, people will want to engage much more frequently.
