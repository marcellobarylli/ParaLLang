## (c) Suggestions

- **Grading:** accept the standard ways to ask "what's wrong" ("Cosa c'è che non va?", "Che cosa c'è?", "Che hai?") and plain "perché ..." / "quindi ..." clauses as reasons. Never surface `INVALID_GRADING` to the player. Fall back to not crediting the objective and continuing the conversation.
- **Tics:** tell the character to use each signature phrase (Ssst, "scrivo anche questo", Primo/Secondo, "Scusa, X") at most once every 3-4 lines, and to vary how it opens and ends its question. Echoing my words is good, but not with the same formula every time.
- **Give-aways:** at A2 and B1, the character should create the situation the objective needs without saying the phrase. Philippa's breakfast nudge is the model. "Is everything all right with the room?" is better than "the window doesn't work". At A1, modelling ("Io mi chiamo... Come ti chiami?") is fine and should stay.

Bene?"), and swap one world detail per turn (rain, a cat being counted) instead of quizzing vocabulary. **Always return a goodbye** with "Arrivederci, Sam!"
