## (a) Top 5 problems

1. **The character says the target phrase first (9 lines, 9 of 9 objectives).** Examples: C1 "Vorrei un tè" before
   the player orders, C2 "Io non so cosa c'è oggi" before the player asks what there is, and P3 "Scusi, non capisco il
   mio libro" right before the "say you don't understand" objective. The player ends up parroting rather than
   producing.
2. **Objective setups that the scene contradicts or never stages (3 of 9).** Marigold "will not be the one to start"
   but opens with "Buonasera, buonasera". Prudence never says "something long, fast and urgent", although her role is
   literally Fast Talker. Septimus is "hovering near your empty plate" while the food is still walking.
3. **Off-level or overlong lines at A1 (5 lines).** C2 "...perché la cucina è sempre indietro sulla strada. Dopo la
   riorganizzazione, è peggio." C6 "prima della riorganizzazione era meglio". G1 "Qui aspetto in fila, da sola.
   Aspetti anche tu?" was the first line a true beginner heard.
4. **Verbal tics repeated within a character (5 lines).** Septimus uses "Uno: ... Due: ..." 3 times (C3, C5, C6).
   Prudence uses "Scrivo nel libro" twice (P4, P5) and "Scusi" 4 times in 3 lines (P1 to P3). Septimus says
   "riorganizzazione" twice.
5. **Echo-the-player openers (7 of 17 lines) and lines with no prompt.** Examples: "Ordinare è giusto.", "Un caffè,
   certo.", "Niente zucchero, allora.", "Sì, sono molto veloce!". C2 and G2 ended with no question or cue, so the
   player had to invent the next step. Smaller issue: P3 ignored "Quanto costa?".

## (b) Best lines

- G6 "Arrivederci, Tom! Questa è la mia ultima parola. Ora la collezione è completa. Buonanotte!" accepts the goodbye,

- G5 "Una fila è questa: io qui, tu dietro di me. Due persone sono già una fila lunga. Capisci?" answers a "non

- G3 "Piacere, Tom! Io sto bene, ma la fila è lunga. Tu come stai?" uses the player's name, is short and has a single
  answerable question.
- C4 "Il pane è già sul tavolo, ma il formaggio cammina ancora. Vuole ancora pane?" is absurd, simple and builds a
  running joke, with an easy yes/no question at the end.
- P4 "Anche io non capisco!" picks up the player's words and turns them into comedy.

## (c) Suggestions at this level

- Stage the objective instead of saying the phrase. For "don't understand", have the Fast Talker actually rattle off a
  long, fast line (marked as such) and wait. For "order politely", ask "Cosa desidera?" without saying "vorrei"
  yourself. For "greet", stay silent or look expectant in the narration when the objective says she won't start.
  Model a phrase only after the player has failed it once.
- Keep A1 lines to 2-3 short clauses of known or cognate words. Drop abstract hooks like "riorganizzazione" and the
  imperfect tense, especially in goodbyes, which should be short.
- Vary the move, not just the words. Avoid a signature structure that recurs every turn ("Uno/Due", "Scrivo nel
  libro"), and do not open by restating the player's line every time. Sometimes react with emotion, sometimes with a
  new event in the scene.
- Always end with one easy cue, a yes/no or either/or question, unless the player said goodbye. Answer the player's
  direct questions (P3's price).
- Keep the weather, absurd objects and character-premise payoffs: they are working, and they work best when the joke
  itself is in A1 Italian.
