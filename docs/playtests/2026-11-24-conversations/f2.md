## (c) Suggestions

- Use the signature gag at most once in the greeting and once more later as a callback (as Tabitha did on her goodbye), not in every line. Vary the form of the line: plain sentences, not the list format every time.
- When an objective is pending, ask the question without first saying the answer frame yourself. Model it only after the learner has tried, or use different vocabulary (e.g. ask "E tu, cosa prendi per cominciare?" without "come antipasto prendo il pane").
- Make sure the objective event happens. If the objective says "she asks whether everything is all right", have her actually ask "Tutto bene?".
- Do not let corrections replace correct learner phrasing with the character's own phrasing. In any case, do not take a heart for it.
- Keep the scene's weather and place fixed within a conversation, or narrate the change.
- De-duplicate across nearby characters: surnames, wish-lists such as the hat fund, the "porta l'ombrello" send-off, and description phrases.
- Keep A2 lines to 2-3 short sentences. Allow inviting remarks (not only questions) when the objective is for the player to ask; this felt the most natural of all.
