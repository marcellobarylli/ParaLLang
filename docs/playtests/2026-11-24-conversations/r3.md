## (a) Top 5 problems

1. **Goodbyes are never honoured. 5 of 5.** The character always appends a new question.
   - Me: "Arrivederci, Lazlo!", Lazlo: "…Un momento, per favore. Dove vai adesso?"
   - Me: "Buonanotte Fergus, a domani!", Fergus: "…Che cosa fai domani mattina?"
   - Me: "Ciao, a presto!", Henrietta: "…Che lavoro fai tu?"
2. **Formulaic reply shape and repeated gags.** The "che bel/bella/buona…" opener appears in 13 of 30 replies, one character tic recurs 4-5 times per conversation, and there are 2 verbatim sentence repeats.
   - "Resti a casa, che bella idea!", then "Il parco, che bella idea!" on the next turn (Fergus)
   - "il timbro aspetta" ×4 (Fergus); "complicata come un grande orologio / come un grande calendario / più complicato di un grande ponte / di un grande trattato" ×4 (Septimus)
   - "Il prosciutto è ottimo come antipasto, e la pasta è ottima come primo", repeated almost word for word in the next reply (Mungo)
3. **The grader rejects correct A2 answers. 3 of 15 graded replies.**
   - "Ieri pioveva molto, tutto il giorno!" was not accepted as "what the weather was like yesterday".
   - "Per cominciare prendo il prosciutto" was not accepted as "what you will have to start with".
   - "Sì, volentieri, mi piacciono le feste!" was not accepted as "accept gladly".
4. **Target phrase handed over in the greeting. 3 clear and 4 mild.**

   - "Qui, di sera, è bene prenotare" (the objective is to book)

## (c) Suggestions for A2 characters

- **Let goodbyes land.** When the player says arrivederci, ciao or a domani, reply with a farewell and a closing quip ("Arrivederci! Il tuo alibi è in triplice copia."). Ask no further question. A final line without a question is fine.
- **Don't end every line with a question.** About 1 in 3 lines could end on a reaction, a confession or a teaser ("Il signore con il cappello… non dice mai il suo nome."). The player then chooses whether to ask, which is how the best threads in this run started.
- **Vary the reaction openers.** Ban the repeated "che bella idea", and use a gag at most twice per conversation. Give each character a few different reactions: surprise, disagreement, a sad note, a counter-claim ("Il cioccolato? No, il limone è più onesto.").
- **Ask for the objective without saying its words.** For example "Hai una domanda per me? Sono un esperto di cielo" instead of "Tutti chiedono che tempo fa". The player should produce the key phrase themselves.
- **React to the player's information first, especially information on the lesson topic.** If the player says it rained yesterday, an umbrella lender should pounce on it.
- **Make the character perform the cue the objective describes** ("Va tutto bene?" for the restaurant complaint, an explicit invitation before "accept gladly").
- **Grader:** accept the imperfetto for past weather, synonyms such as "per cominciare" for "to start with", and glad acceptances without "con piacere".
- **Keep the good things.** Answering the player's questions, paying off narration details, weather woven in through the job, and one absurd but clear rule per character.
