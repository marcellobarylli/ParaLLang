## (a) Top 5 problems

1. **Two or more questions per line, nearly always: 29 of 30 character lines.** Only conversation 1 R2 asked a single
   question. Openers often ask 3 or 4 questions.
   - "E tu, come ti chiami, come stai oggi e che cosa pensi della mia porta?"
   - "Raccontami di te: da dove arrivi, dove stai di solito, come parli con me e che cosa pensi del mio lavoro?"
   - "Che cosa desidera da me oggi? E lei, che lavoro fa, e che cosa pensa del mio lavoro?"

2. **Self-centred "my work / my door" questions: about 23 of 30 lines**, about 19 of them a near-identical "che cosa
   vuoi sapere del mio lavoro?" or "che cosa pensi del mio lavoro?". They appear in every conversation and in every
   keep-chatting turn without exception (10 of 10). A1 learners cannot answer them.
   - "che cosa pensi della mia porta?" (twice in a row, conversation 1)
   - "Che cosa pensi del mio lavoro, e chi speri di incontrare oggi?"
   - "Come sono i tuoi studenti, e che cosa vuoi sapere del mio lavoro?"

3. **Off-task, off-level questions once the objectives are done (owner complaint 2).** After resolution, every
   follow-up question needed vocabulary the learner doesn't have. That was 18 of 18 post-objective lines across the
   five conversations ("cosa fai", "dove vai", "cosa mangi", "chi speri di incontrare", "come passi la serata"). The
   characters also pull in next-unit material ("da dove vieni" in a greetings lesson). Long side clauses turn up in
   about 15 of 30 lines.
   - "Non c'è, ma è in pulizia da molti anni. E tu, che cosa fai oggi sotto la pioggia?"
   - "Prima delle nove riorganizzazioni tutto era meglio, ma questa è un'altra storia."
   - "Di solito dico «arrivederci» a chi non torna. Che cosa fai oggi, e che cosa pensi della strada?"

4. **Characters refuse or ignore the goodbye, even in the hello/goodbye unit.** I said arrivederci 7 times. It was
   refused 5 times, ignored once, and accepted but then followed by more questions once. It was never answered with a
   simple "Arrivederci, Tom!".

   - "I will not stamp one while the lightning is still going. Stai qui un momento, va bene?"
   - "Tu dici «arrivederci», ma io non ho ancora finito di contare."

   - "Uh... non capisco. Come stai?" was "corrected" to the identical text, and I lost a heart.

- Bartholomew: "Prego, prego." It is a natural, tiny, level-appropriate reply to "grazie".
- Marigold: "Piacere, Tom... va così così... non c'è male". It gives variations on "bene", exactly what the owner asks for.

## (c) Suggestions for the character at A1

- **One short question per turn, and it should be about the player or the task:** "E tu, come stai?", "Come ti
  chiami?", "Di dove sei?". Never ask "what do you think of my X" or "what do you want to know about my work".
- **Keep sentences to about 6-10 words and 2-3 sentences per turn.** Recycle the unit's words and give one or two
  variations at a time (piacere, così così, non c'è male, a presto, anch'io). Put the absurd humour in a short tag
  ("dodici: che delusione!") or in the narration, not in subordinate clauses.
- **After the objectives are done, keep practising the same words.** Ask the reverse question ("E io? Chiedimi come
  sto!"), greet a passing cat, play a variation. Do not open new topics (food, plans, jobs) the learner has no words for.
- **Accept the goodbye at A1.** Answer it with a model ("Arrivederci, Tom! A presto!") and let the scene end
  gracefully. A short running gag is fine ("Arrivederci! ...the door says ciao too").
- **When the learner says "non capisco", rephrase more simply or repeat one key word.** Do not pile on new content.
- **Grader:** accept "Bene, grazie" and other correct short forms. Never cost a heart when the correction equals the
  input. Use "Sono inglese" / "Vengo dall'Inghilterra" as models. Credit objectives the first time they are met.
- **Objectives:** if the character must wait to be greeted, have the opener be narration plus a gesture, not a full
  greeting.
- **Immersion 0.2 worked well for A1 comprehension** (the Italian landed on the target words). Consider it, or
  something like 0.4, as the default for the first units.
