# Round 216 findings

## Question

How should a new player's starting share of the language be set, and which tiers are usual?

## Finding

The CEFR self-assessment grid ([Europass copy](https://europass.europa.eu/system/files/2020-05/CEFR%20self-assessment%20grid%20EN.pdf))
describes A1 as familiar words and very basic phrases and A2 as simple questions on familiar topics, which fit the
owner's three tiers. The 30 % of the lowest tier is this round's choice (the slider's minimum is 10 %, the curriculum's
first stage is 20 %).

Tested: client suite 382 pass; existing screen tests pick the top tier before the journey. **Not seen in a browser or
on a phone**, and no new unit test of its own beyond those flows.
