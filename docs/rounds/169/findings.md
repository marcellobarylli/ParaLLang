# Round 169 findings

## Question

Does the lore as written leave room for the followers of the void to be right?

## Finding

Not quite, before this round: one join of round 167 had already judged them. It does now. The book's own part VI
turns out to be the "early telling" the owner describes, and it already holds one line of the other side.

## Method

The lore book read against the owner's message; the particulars of Dark Souls looked up before being written.

## Decision

- **My own earlier join is corrected, and the book says so.** It had taken a side.
- **Part VI is left as it is.** It is the compiler's voice, and the compiler believing that nothing grows after
  Collision is now useful: it is the early telling. The line "the end of one world is, some say, the seed of
  another" was already there.
- **Nothing is put into the game.** The owner asked for it to be added and pushed, not built. What is built today
  puts neither case.
