  it starts at 100 %. It replaces the share the content sets (a character's own share, rising each turn) wherever
  the web client talks to a character: the road, the dungeons, the party.

  language dropped in, as round 130 made it.

  sentences share colours with the line (round 142) is what makes a line in full Italian readable. Today the
  translation of the line being answered is behind "Translate sentence", which spends a companion hint and lowers
  the mark of the words answered, and a gate allows no hints at all. With lines in full Italian that would leave a
  beginner unable to read the question. So at 100 % the translation shows with the line, free.
- **"Keep voice acting."** The voices read the line in the language's own voice, which suits a line that is wholly
  in it. Nothing in the voices changes.
